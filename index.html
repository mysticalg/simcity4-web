This reconstruction uses the archives from your own copy of SimCity 4 Deluxe. Select the installed game folder, including its Regions folder.
Your files stay on your computer and are read locally in your browser.
Have a GOG installer? Extract or install it first. The installer .exe cannot run in the browser.
