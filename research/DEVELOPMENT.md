## Current behavior

Residential/commercial offer three densities; industrial offers medium/high. Unbuilt lots use source recipes compatible with every tile's zone type. Original stage weights are evaluated against the current live cohort census; selection is deterministic across saves and varies with coordinates/month. Growth requires dry land, road access and power; stages above the native water cap require active service over the footprint. City population no longer implies an automatic stage increment every month.

Higher-stage developments can replace/assemble owned plots when the selected building has greater capacity than the buildings removed. Any unused portions of removed parcels remain zoned with their previous density. Existing original-city lots are not automatically redeveloped yet. Rezoning owned buildings changes individual ground tiles without deleting the building; later growth checks all those densities. Version-8 saves preserve per-tile density and per-city tool selection. Older saves receive their original low-R/C and medium-I defaults.

## Evidence and limitations

Native fixtures check all developer counts/settings, population breakpoints, original prices, stage-8 eligibility with sufficient population/water, dry-stage limits, land/road/power gates and parcel assembly across a partially rezoned source footprint. The supplied Making Money tutorial has 1,831 saved R$ occupants. The browser scenario grows a new district to stage 3 without local water; adding a powered original pump and pipes permits a native stage-4 apartment building. It verifies original prices, zoning-area preservation, partial rezoning without building loss, native artwork, and save/reload.

The curves, recipes, costs and cap interpretation are native evidence. Weighted selection, candidate ordering, parcel rebuilding and monthly scheduling are reconstructed. Native regional census adjustments, tract allocation, desirability, real demand, occupation timing, construction timers, commutes, workforce/education and wealth transitions are unfinished. New growth remains R$, CS$ and dirty industry. Agricultural compound lots/field ownership and their zoning tool remain unfinished; agriculture metadata is indexed but not exposed as a completed tool. Native source-city density editing, historical building controls, foundations/automatic grading and full stage-distribution parity also remain outstanding. This release does not complete the original game simulation.
