# Original growable lot reconstruction — v0.11.0

The compiler reads 1,733 growable lot recipes with resolvable original building references from SimCity_1.dat and EP1.dat, preserving cohort inheritance. LotConfigPropertySize (88edc790), GrowthStage (27812837), ZoneTypes (88edc793), wealth (88edc795) and LotObject properties 88edc900 onward supply the layout. Building/prop family 27812870 is resolved against exemplar IIDs and family members. Source archives stay unchanged and are not published; the existing bundled resource packs contain the model/texture payloads.

LotObject arrays retain type, LOD flags, orientation, fixed-point coordinates and bounds (1/65536 metres), and alternative IIDs. Types 0/1/2 supply buildings/static props/textures. Texture FSH group 0986135e uses the highest detail variant (+4) when present; UV orientation/mirroring is retained. Original model zoom/rotation variants and night overlays are used. Source model bounds are positioned within the encoded object bounds. Texture squares follow terrain triangles. This preserves the native composition without adding fabricated buildings or garden art.

New growth now chooses source stage-one lots for low-wealth residential, low-wealth commercial services and dirty industry (zone types 1/4/8). It requires powered land, a complete contiguous rectangle of the same unbuilt zone and road access along the front edge. Footprints rotate toward roads. Selection and alternative building/prop choices are deterministic from plot coordinates, providing varied developments across a district. Constituent zone cells become one indexed multi-tile lot. Utility consumption, capacity, assessed value and demolition costs use that chosen building exemplar. Native scenery is cleared across the entire new footprint.

Version-6 snapshots preserve recipe, building IID, orientation, seed and footprint; versions 1–5 remain readable. Legacy buildings retain their legacy size-band representation. Any footprint tile can query/demolish the whole development. New native lots do not automatically climb the old three provisional capacity bands.

## Validation

The bundled-fixture unit check validates all recipe count metadata, a real 1x3 residential recipe with textures/props, building-family resolution and development beside all four road edges. Occupancy, missing road and power gates are checked. The browser mixed-district scenario produced eight native lots: four residential, two commercial, two industrial, with population 23/jobs 124. Every selected supported texture/model loaded without a rendering error. Day/night captures were visually inspected, and orientation, varied models, stage stability, save/restore and demolition from an interior footprint tile were verified. The map-tool mouse test and existing native utility/finance checks remain regression coverage.

## Remaining reconstruction

The lot recipes and art are original; lot selection is reconstructed rather than a translation of the native developer algorithm. Full demand/desirability, occupancy/commute simulation, density selection, developer stage probabilities, redevelopment, water-dependent stage limits and construction timers are still missing. Growth currently admits only stage one and the initial wealth/industry subsets above. It does not claim all indexed recipes are playable. Agricultural fields and higher-wealth lots are not selected. Time/season-dependent props, effects/animation, flora/fences/water/network LotObject types and native foundation grading are unfinished. Static prop alternatives are resolved, but native probability and scheduling rules are not reproduced. Debug lotArt reports loaded textures/models, failures and skipped objects; skipped objects must not be described as fully rendered native content.
